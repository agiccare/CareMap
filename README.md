# CareMap
ACGT CareMap is a proposed AI-assisted, human-governed geographic knowledge infrastructure designed to make the nation's fragmented elder-care and longevity ecosystem transparent, trustworthy, and accessible. Modelled after traditional property research companies, the project prioritizes building a meticulously verified database before scaling software or expanding into broader life-course care domains. The initiative operates as an independent information and navigation platform, keeping basic public search completely free while generating sustainable enterprise revenue through professional tools, spatial intelligence, and institutional data services. To ensure comprehensive financial security, the ecosystem also incorporates professionally administered care trusts and structured partnerships with authorized trustees, while maintaining strict separation from real estate development assets. Ultimately, the venture seeks to establish a durable national industry reference that bridges families, care providers, and communities across Malaysia.
